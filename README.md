# BigCountry's RF Lab

An independent RF engineering lab focused on software-defined radio, satellite communications, antenna development, and signal processing.

## About the Lab

BigCountry's RF Lab documents hands-on engineering experiments, from initial design and testing through data collection, analysis, and iterative improvements.

The goal is to develop practical RF engineering skills while maintaining a public record of experimental methods, results, challenges, and lessons learned.

## Current Projects

### Weather Satellite Ground Station

Receiving and decoding meteorological satellite transmissions using an RTL-SDR receiver and portable antenna system.

**Satellites received:**
- METEOR-M2-4 — Daytime LRPT imagery
- METEOR-M2-3 — Nighttime infrared imagery

### Antenna Development

Experimenting with antenna configurations and reception techniques to improve signal quality and satellite coverage.

## Technical Areas

- Software-defined radio (SDR)
- Satellite communications
- RF reception and antenna systems
- Digital signal processing
- Experimental testing and documentation
